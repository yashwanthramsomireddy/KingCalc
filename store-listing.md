```
KingCalc is a clean, fast calculator for everyday life. Calculate, convert currencies and units, work out percentages, and plan a loan EMI, all in one small app.

It is free for everyone. No ads. No tracking. No account. No in-app purchases.

CALCULATOR
• Live answer preview as you type
• Tap the expression to place a blinking cursor and edit anywhere
• Copy a formula or its answer, and paste formulas back in
• Smart % (200 + 10% = 220), smart brackets, and a 00 key
• Square root, square, power and π
• History of your calculations, kept only on your phone
• Works in portrait and landscape

CURRENCY CONVERTER
• About 160 world currencies
• Favourites, search and one-tap swap
• Works offline with the last saved rates

UNIT CONVERTER
• Length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time and data
• See every unit at once
• Indian area units included: cent, guntha and ground

PERCENT
• X% of Y, X is what % of Y, and % change

LOAN / EMI
• Monthly EMI, total interest and total payable
• Full month-by-month payment schedule

MADE YOUR WAY
• Pitch-black AMOLED theme, White theme, or follow your phone
• Five fonts: Inter, Poppins, JetBrains Mono, Space Grotesk and Nunito
• Indian (1,00,000) or international (100,000) number grouping
• Adjustable decimal places
• Built-in FAQ that explains every feature

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
KingCalc collects no personal data. Your history and settings stay on your device. The only internet request is downloading currency rates, and it contains none of your data.

OPEN SOURCE
The full source code is free on GitHub under the MIT license: github.com/yashwanthramsomireddy/KingCalc

Currency rates are for information only and are not suitable for financial transactions. EMI results are estimates; your lender's figures may differ slightly.

Built by Yashwanth Ram Somireddy, a TeamExyKings product.
Questions or ideas? Email Yashwanthramsomireddy@gmail.com
```

## Phone screenshots (2 minimum, 8 maximum)
Take these from the app installed through Google Play or the release APK, not from Expo Go, so no Expo icon appears.

Play requires each side between 320 and 3840 px, and the long side may be at most 2x the short side. Very tall phones (like the S22 Ultra, ratio about 2.14) exceed that, so crop or pad to 9:16 (for example 1080 x 1920) before uploading.

Suggested order and captions:
1. Calculator, Black theme, showing a formula such as `12,500 × 18%`. Caption: "Fast, clean and private"
2. Calculator with the cursor placed in the middle of a formula. Caption: "Edit anywhere with the cursor"
3. Currency, for example USD to INR. Caption: "160 currencies, works offline"
4. Units, Area (sq ft to guntha and cent). Caption: "Every unit at a glance"
5. Percent screen. Caption: "Percent made simple"
6. Loan / EMI with a result showing. Caption: "EMI, interest and full schedule"
7. Calculator in White theme. Caption: "Black or White, your choice"
8. Calculator in landscape. Caption: "Landscape ready"

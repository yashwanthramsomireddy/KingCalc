# Privacy Policy for KingCalc

**Effective date:** 2026-09-28
**Developer:** Yashwanth Ram Somireddy, TeamExyKings

## Summary

KingCalc does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. It has no account system, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

## Data stored on your device

KingCalc saves the following locally, on your phone only:

- Your calculation history
- Your settings (theme, font, decimal places, number format)
- Your favourite currencies
- The most recent currency exchange rates, so the converter works offline

This data never leaves your device. You can clear your history in Settings at any time, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

## Data we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, files, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID.

## Network requests

The only network request KingCalc makes is to fetch currency exchange rates from the free ExchangeRate-API open-access service (open.er-api.com).

- The request does not include any personal information or data you have entered.
- As with any internet request, the rates service can see your device's IP address. We do not receive, log or use this information. Their handling is governed by their own policy.
- If you never open the Currency screen, no request is made. If you are offline, the app uses the last saved rates.

## Permissions

KingCalc requests no sensitive permissions. It uses internet access only for the currency rates described above, and vibration for optional key-press feedback.

## Third-party services

- **ExchangeRate-API (currency rates):** described above. Their terms and privacy practices apply to that request: https://www.exchangerate-api.com/terms
- **No** advertising networks, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs are included.

## Google Play

KingCalc is distributed through Google Play. Google may share anonymous, aggregated statistics with the developer, such as install counts and crash reports from users who chose to share diagnostics. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. KingCalc itself does not collect it, and the app has no way to identify you.

## Children's privacy

KingCalc collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
